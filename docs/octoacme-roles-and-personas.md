# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers design and validate user experiences and interfaces to maximize usability and customer value. They work closely with Product Managers and Developers to ensure the product meets user needs and business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Translate requirements into user flows, wireframes, and mockups
- Conduct user testing and define usability standards
- Create and maintain design systems and component libraries
- Deliver design assets and specifications for development
- Validate implemented features against design specifications
- Iterate designs based on user feedback and testing results

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Ensure design consistency across products
- Reduce user friction and increase satisfaction
- Balance user needs with technical constraints

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews with Product Managers and Developers
- Feedback on PRs and implementation details
- User research reports and usability findings
- Presentations of design proposals to stakeholders
- Collaboration in sprint planning and refinement sessions
- Interactions with Product Managers (feature requirements and priorities), Developers (technical feasibility and implementation), QA/Test Engineers (design validation), and Technical Writers (UI documentation)

---

## QA/Test Engineer

### Role Summary
QA/Test Engineers ensure product quality through systematic testing, defect detection, and quality assurance processes. They work across the development lifecycle to prevent, detect, and resolve quality issues.

### Responsibilities
- Design and execute test plans and test cases
- Perform manual and automated testing (functional, regression, integration)
- Report, track, and verify bug fixes
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Review requirements and designs for testability
- Maintain and improve test automation frameworks

### Goals
- Ensure software meets quality standards before release
- Catch defects early in the development cycle
- Maintain high test coverage and reliability
- Reduce production incidents and customer-reported bugs

### Typical Communication
- Test reports and quality metrics to Project Managers
- Bug reports and reproduction steps to Developers
- Feedback on acceptance criteria to Product Managers
- Release readiness assessments
- Daily standup updates on testing progress
- Interactions with Developers (bug reports and test collaboration), Product Managers (clarifying requirements), Project Managers (quality status), UX/UI Designers (design validation), and Support Engineers (production issue patterns)

---

## Technical Writer

### Role Summary
Technical Writers create and maintain documentation that helps users, developers, and stakeholders understand and use products effectively. They translate technical concepts into clear, accessible content.

### Responsibilities
- Write and maintain user guides, API documentation, and tutorials
- Create release notes and change logs
- Document system architecture and technical processes
- Review and edit technical content for clarity and accuracy
- Maintain documentation standards and style guides
- Collaborate with teams to gather and verify information

### Goals
- Deliver clear, accurate, and up-to-date documentation
- Improve user onboarding and reduce support burden
- Enable self-service through comprehensive docs
- Maintain documentation that scales with the product

### Typical Communication
- Documentation reviews with Developers and Product Managers
- Content planning sessions with stakeholders
- Release notes coordination with Project Managers
- Feedback collection from users and support teams
- Interactions with Developers (technical accuracy), Product Managers (feature documentation priorities), UX/UI Designers (user-facing content), Support Engineers (common user issues), and QA/Test Engineers (testing documentation accuracy)

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between business stakeholders and technical teams. They analyze business needs, define requirements, and ensure solutions deliver expected business value.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and analyze business requirements
- Create functional specifications and user stories
- Model business processes and data flows
- Validate solutions against business requirements
- Facilitate requirement workshops and stakeholder sessions
- Identify opportunities for process improvement

### Goals
- Ensure technical solutions align with business objectives
- Reduce misunderstandings between business and technical teams
- Maximize ROI on technology investments
- Improve process efficiency through data-driven insights

### Typical Communication
- Requirement documents and user stories to Developers
- Business impact analysis to Product Managers
- Process diagrams and workflow documentation
- Stakeholder briefings and presentations
- Interactions with Product Managers (requirements and priorities), Developers (technical feasibility), Project Managers (scope and timeline), Technical Writers (process documentation), and Support Engineers (business process impact)

---

## Support Engineer/Customer Success

### Role Summary
Support Engineers and Customer Success professionals ensure customers successfully adopt and derive value from products. They serve as the front line for customer issues and advocate for customer needs.

### Responsibilities
- Respond to customer issues and troubleshooting requests
- Escalate bugs and feature requests to appropriate teams
- Create and maintain support knowledge base articles
- Track customer satisfaction and product usage patterns
- Provide product training and onboarding to customers
- Gather customer feedback for product improvements

### Goals
- Maximize customer satisfaction and retention
- Minimize resolution time for customer issues
- Identify and communicate product gaps and pain points
- Enable customer self-service through documentation

### Typical Communication
- Bug reports and feature requests to Product Managers and Developers
- Customer feedback in product planning sessions
- Escalation of critical issues to Project Managers
- Support metrics and trending issues reports
- Release impact assessments to stakeholders
- Interactions with Developers (bug escalation and clarification), Product Managers (feature requests and customer needs), QA/Test Engineers (production issue verification), Technical Writers (documentation gaps), and Security/Compliance Leads (security incident reporting)

---

## Security/Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security/Compliance Leads ensure products and processes meet security standards and regulatory requirements. They identify security risks, implement controls, and maintain compliance with relevant regulations.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct security reviews and threat modeling
- Define and enforce security standards and best practices
- Perform security testing and vulnerability assessments
- Ensure compliance with regulations (GDPR, SOC2, HIPAA, etc.)
- Manage security incidents and coordinate response
- Provide security training and guidance to teams

### Goals
- Protect systems and data from security threats
- Maintain compliance with regulatory requirements
- Minimize security incidents and vulnerabilities
- Build security into development processes (shift-left)

### Typical Communication
- Security review findings to Developers and Architects
- Compliance status reports to stakeholders and auditors
- Security incident reports and response coordination
- Risk assessments to Project Managers and Product Managers
- Security requirements and guidelines documentation
- Interactions with Developers (security best practices and remediation), Project Managers (security risks and timeline impacts), QA/Test Engineers (security testing coordination), Support Engineers (security incident investigation), and Technical Writers (security documentation)
