# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management documentation! This serves as your central hub for understanding how we manage projects from initiation to continuous improvement.

## Overview

OctoAcme follows an **iterative, outcome-driven approach** to project management that emphasizes delivering customer value through incremental releases. Our methodology combines structured processes with flexibility, allowing teams to adapt while maintaining clear accountability and transparency.

We believe that great project management enables teams to focus on what matters most: building products and services that delight our customers and drive business impact.

## Key Principles

Our project management approach is built on these foundational principles:

### Clear Stakeholder Alignment
- Every project has identified sponsors, stakeholders, and a clear problem statement
- Regular communication cadences ensure everyone stays informed
- Decisions are documented and shared transparently

### Living Documentation
- Project plans, risk registers, and decision logs are maintained throughout the project lifecycle
- Documentation evolves with the project and serves as institutional knowledge
- Use of `.copilot/` directories enables AI-assisted context and guidance

### Incremental Delivery and Review
- Work is broken into shippable increments with clear acceptance criteria
- Iterative reviews and demos keep stakeholders engaged
- Early and frequent releases reduce risk and enable faster feedback

### Transparent Roles and Responsibilities
- Every project has a named Project Manager and Product Lead
- Team members understand their responsibilities and decision-making authority
- Cross-functional collaboration is encouraged and supported

### Continuous Improvement
- Retrospectives capture learnings and drive process improvements
- Data-informed decisions guide iteration and optimization
- Psychological safety encourages honest feedback and experimentation

## High-Level Project Lifecycle

OctoAcme projects follow a five-phase lifecycle:

### 1. Initiation
Define the problem, align stakeholders, and create a project one-pager with success metrics and initial timeline. The goal is to validate the business need and secure go-ahead for detailed planning.

### 2. Planning
Break work into a prioritized, estimated backlog with clear acceptance criteria. Identify dependencies, risks, and create a release plan with milestones.

### 3. Execution & Tracking
Build, test, and iterate on features through sprints. Maintain visibility through standups, weekly syncs, and project board updates. Actively manage risks and dependencies.

### 4. Release & Deployment
Deploy features to production with proper verification, rollback plans, and announcements. Ensure successful handoff to operations and support teams.

### 5. Retrospective & Continuous Improvement
Capture learnings, celebrate successes, and identify process improvements. Document outcomes and close out project artifacts.

## Getting Started for New Team Members

### If you're joining an existing project:
1. Review the **[Project Management Overview](octoacme-project-management-overview.md)** to understand our core principles
2. Read the **[Roles and Personas](octoacme-roles-and-personas.md)** guide to understand your role and responsibilities
3. Use the **[Persona Onboarding Checklist](persona-onboarding-checklist.md)** to ensure a complete onboarding experience
4. Locate your project's charter or one-pager (usually in the project repo's `docs/` or `.copilot/` folder)
5. Review the project board and backlog to understand current priorities
6. Attend the next standup or sync meeting to introduce yourself

### If you're starting a new project:
1. Begin with **[Project Initiation](octoacme-project-initiation.md)** to create your project one-pager
2. Follow the initiation checklist to secure stakeholder alignment
3. Move to **[Project Planning](octoacme-project-planning.md)** once you receive go-ahead
4. Set up your project repository and add relevant process docs to `.copilot/` for AI-assisted guidance

### General Tips:
- Keep documentation living and up-to-date - don't let it become stale
- Use the issue templates (see below) to request process improvements
- Lean on your Project Manager and Product Lead for guidance
- Participate actively in retrospectives to help improve our processes

## Key Artifacts and Templates

OctoAcme projects use consistent artifacts to maintain quality and transparency:

### Core Project Artifacts
- **Project Charter / One-pager** - Concise problem statement, goals, metrics, and stakeholders
- **Roadmap and Release Plan** - Timeline showing major milestones and releases
- **Sprint/Iteration Backlog** - Prioritized work items with acceptance criteria
- **Definition of Done** - Quality standards and completion criteria
- **Risk Register** - Tracked risks with impact, probability, and mitigation plans
- **Retrospective Notes** - Learnings and action items from retrospectives

## Communication Cadence

Regular communication keeps projects on track:

- **Daily Standups** - Quick sync within delivery team (typically 15 minutes)
- **Weekly PM + Product Lead Sync** - Alignment on priorities and blockers
- **Bi-weekly Sprint Planning & Review** - Backlog grooming and demo sessions
- **Monthly Stakeholder Updates** - Progress reports and roadmap reviews
- **As-needed Escalations** - Risk mitigation and urgent decision-making
